# Study Streak · PAU 400L SEN

Short lessons, active recall, spaced review, timed mocks and a daily streak for every 400L Software Engineering exam (1st semester 2026/27).

Open `index.html` in a browser to use it. Progress saves in your browser; back it up from the Me page.

On the hosted site, use the search box or press **Ctrl+K** to find lessons, recall prompts, quizzes and past-paper questions across courses. Missed objective questions are saved under **Weak spots** for a focused retry. Cheat sheets and past papers have a **Print / Save PDF** button.

The app includes a web app manifest and service worker. After its first visit on the hosted site, the app shell and course data are available offline and can be installed from the browser menu. Opening the HTML directly from disk does not enable service workers; serve the folder over HTTPS or localhost for install and offline caching.

All eight courses are listed under **Courses**, including electives that are not part of the current study plan. Each course page links to its study guide or available study materials. Word guides can be downloaded and opened in Word or a compatible app; PDFs can be opened in the browser or downloaded. SEN 419 currently has topic PDFs rather than a single complete study guide.

New: **📥 Guides** collects every downloadable study guide plus a printable full guide per course. Each course page adds **🎓 Sit it timed** (the real past paper under exam time, then self-marking), **✍️ Essay trainer** (Background → Answer and solution → Scenario → How they combine → Conclusion), confidence dots for each topic and a **🌙 Exam-eve sheet** that puts shaky topics first. Tap 🌓 for dark mode.
