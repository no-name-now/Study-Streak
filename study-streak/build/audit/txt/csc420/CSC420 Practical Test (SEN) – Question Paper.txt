Useful facts about the data: 
• An order may be delivered in several shipments. Each element of deliveries is the 
quantity delivered in one shipment. 
• Total delivered = sum of all elements in deliveries. An order is short-delivered if its 
total delivered is less than quantity_ordered. 
• Orders from 2026 are still open: only their first shipment has been recorded. 
• manager_id in products is the employee who manages that product.
