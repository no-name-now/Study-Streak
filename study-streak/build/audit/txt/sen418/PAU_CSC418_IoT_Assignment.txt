Overview & Instructions 
Select ONE domain scenario below. Your solutions must demonstrate clear end-to-end system thinking 
moving from physical perception to edge processing, network routing 
Scenario Options (Select One) 
Option A: Smart City & Environmental Safety — River Flood Early Warning System 
Describe how you will design an automated real-time river flood detection and warning infrastructure deployed 
across flood-prone urban watercourses to protect human life, civil infrastructure, and urban property.  
Option B: Smart Logistics— Real-Time Food Shipment Monitoring 
Describe how you will design an end-to-end IoT tracking solution integrated into refrigerated shipping containers 
to continuously monitor environmental conditions and dynamically re -route sensitive food produce before 
spoilage occurs. 
Option C: Precision Agriculture — Automated Greenhouse & Smart Irrigation 
Describe how you will design an intelligent greenhouse micro-climate control and smart irrigation system that 
monitors soil and atmospheric metrics to maximize crop yields while optimizing water and energy resource 
consumption. 
Important: 
• Sensors: Identify specific sensors required for your selected scenario.  
• Actuators / Feedback: Specify one actuator or automated dynamic control device triggered by system outputs 
to modify environmental conditions or alert operators. 
• Threshold Parameters: Define the operational threshold limits (e.g., critical moisture level, temperature 
ceiling, water level offset) that trigger automated edge interventions. 
• Structure a multi-layer IoT architectural pipeline detailing how data flows across all operational layers: 
Perception Layer (Sensors) -> Edge/Gateway Node -> Network Protocol -> Cloud Backend / Dashboard. 
• Describe the explicit connectivity technologies (e.g., LoRaWAN, Cellular, MQTT, CoAP) utilized to convey 
sensor measurements from rural/field nodes to centralized cloud servers and web/mobile user applications.  
• Evaluate how your proposed IoT architecture outperforms traditional, non -automated manual processes.
