Takeaways
• AI will be integrated into healthcare in a 
variety of ways. 
• AI stands to impact healthcare at all levels, 
from the individual, to the institutional, to the 
societal. 
• Ethical assessment requires understanding the 
way AI raises epistemic challenges and the 
potential impacts at all organizational levels.
