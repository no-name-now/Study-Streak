 A part of values by design approach
 The goal is to ultimately maximize the positive impacts of tech 
and minimize any potential negative effects on the society
 It is the intentional embedding of ethical and humane use 
principles into the process of designing, developing, and 
delivering our software and services.
 It is not  a method but an approach but can be incorporated in 
any design methodology.
 Values an be drawn from UN charter on human rights.
 Values can be drawn from human flourishing perspectives, virtues 
ethics, fundamental ethical principles
