What is computing ethics 
“On my view, computer ethics is the analysis of the 
nature and social impact of computer technology and 
the corresponding formulation and justification of 
policies for the ethical use of such technology. I use the 
phrase "computer technology" because I take the 
subject matter of the field broadly to include computers 
and associated technology. For instance, I include 
concerns about software as well as hardware and 
concerns about networks connecting computers as well 
as computers themselves.
What other aspects should be included in computing

Case 1: A major tech company develops an AI system 
to screen job applicants. After deployment, data shows 
the system consistently ranks male candidates higher 
than equally qualified female candidates.

epidemic, the company is asked to share users’ private

swerving to avoid pedestrians (risking the passenger’s

Descriptive Ethics vs Normative Ethics

--- page 12 ---
Descriptive Ethics
• Examples of Descriptive Ethical Inquiry
– What do companies believe is ethical to use 
client’s data for?
– What are the established norms that companies 
follow in research and development of AI 
technologies?
– What do engineers think is permissible to do to 
get more people to use their products? 
– Does the public view AI as being used largely 
ethically or unethically?
--- page 13 ---
Normative Ethics
• Examples of Normative Ethical Inquiry
– Should we have laws that specify who should have 
access to people’s digital medical data? 
– Should we regulate the creation and access to 
online data about people’s medical visits?
– Is using AI to predict whether an individual is likely 
to recommit crimes just or fair?
– Should programmers refuse to develop 
autonomous weapon systems? 
– Should a programmer be held responsible for the 
harm caused by an app they made?
--- page 14 ---
Laws vs Codes of Conduct vs Moral Principles
• Laws
– Anti-discrimination law
– Privacy laws
• Codes of Conduct
– ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct
– Code of Ethics for Engineers
• Moral Principles
– It is wrong to develop applications that exacerbate 
social inequality. 
--- page 15 ---
Ethical Questions at Different Levels of Social Organization
• General/Abstract
– What is rightness?
– What is fairness or justice?
• Political
– What laws should govern the use of (specific) AI 
technologies/applications and data collection and use?
• Interpersonal
– Which decisions should we turn over to algorithmic 
decision-making? 
• Individual
– How should I interact with algorithmic decision-making 
tools? 
--- page 16 ---
Ethical Questions at Different Levels of Social Organization
• These are all distinct questions and their 
relationships non-obvious:
– Should the state prohibit companies from using 
consumer data to personalize news feeds?
– Should companies use consumer data to 
personalize news feeds?
– Should I get my news from a website that 
personalizes my news feed based on consumer 
data?
– Should I work to prevent others from getting news 
from a website that personalizes my news feed 
based on consumer data?

Takeaways
• There are important differences between 
normative and descriptive ethical inquiry.
• Ethical norms are not solely or purely a 
function of what is mandated by law or 
embedded in codes of conduct.
• Careful normative inquiry requires 
distinguishing between various subjects of 
assessment.
