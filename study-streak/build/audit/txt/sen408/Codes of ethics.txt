Group learning: Three tasks
Using the code of ethics for your group:
A. Look for real life examples events, news or 
stories (connected to your document/topic) 
that demonstrate the need to have codes of 
conduct for professionals in the Tech space
B. Choose one ethical issue or a violation of a 
specific code of conduct within your example 
(newsfeed) and analyse it using one or more 
of the foundational topics we discussed 
earlier in the course: Fundamental ethical 
principles, virtues, VSD, HCE etc
C. Post your findings on the Forum and ensure 
that you read and comment on ideas from at 
least one group other than yours within the 
next one week

2. Cybercrime act
3. IEEE code of ethics
4. Trademark act (Nigeria)
5. Computer Professionals of Nigeria 
(registration council) code of 
conduct
