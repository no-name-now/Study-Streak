Why Deployment Matters
● Building an app is only the ﬁrst half — it only delivers value when users can 
access it.
● Deployment is the bridge between “it works on my machine” and “real users 
on real devices”.
● Mistakes in deployment cost time, money, user trust.

What is deployment?
Deployment refers to the process of making your application available to your 
users.
Web app deployment
making a web application available on the internet so users can interact with it.
Mobile app deployment
delivering a mobile app to devices and making it available for use.

Deployment 
Strategies.
--- page 14 ---
Best Practices
 ● Use version control (Git). 
● Use environment variables, separate dev/staging/production conﬁgs.
● Automate builds and deployments (CI/CD).
● Minimise manual steps to reduce error.
● Monitor performance and errors in production.
● Rollback strategy: know how to revert.
● Security: HTTPS, CORS, access controls.
● For mobile: handle store updates, versioning, clear changelog
--- page 15 ---
Common Pitfalls
 ● “Works on my machine” but fails in production because conﬁgs diﬀer.
● Manual deployments cause drift and human error.
● Neglecting rollback or hotﬁx planning.
● Ignoring monitoring so you don’t know when things break.
● Deploying without testing/staging environment.
● For mobile: releasing bad version, missing store rules, fragmented devices 
causing bugs.

Things to consider
 ● Deployment isn’t the end. Y ou need to monitor performance, track errors,
